## One-liner

Lazarus Deal Recovery helps sales managers and VPs of Sales run cleaner forecast calls — which deals will actually close, what’s blocking the others, and what to do next.

## Short blurb (≤80 words)

Keep Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, HubSpot, and Salesforce. Lazarus Deal Recovery is the judgment layer on top: it tells managers which stalled deals are recoverable versus a flat no, names the blocker, and hands back a CRM-ready next step. Human-led, AI-assisted. Not an AI SDR, not a recorder bot, not an autonomous closer.

## Long description

Sales managers can see which deals are stalled in CRM. They cannot always defend the forecast — which rep-owned deals will close, which are recoverable versus a flat no, and what to clear before the next pipeline review.

Lazarus Deal Recovery is a revenue intelligence layer for mid-market B2B teams. Reps and managers bring evidence from the tools they already use: a call, a live meeting transcript, a mailbox thread they search, or a CRM note they import. Lazarus returns close likelihood, root cause, and a 0–90 day recovery plan managers can paste into HubSpot or Salesforce.

**What customers authorize**

- Sign in with Google is identity only (name and email). It is not Gmail, Calendar, or Meet access.
- Optional connectors are user-initiated and least-privilege: read a mailbox thread they search, import a deal they pick, or stream live captions they turned on.
- Live Meet captions use a Chrome extension on Meet’s own captions. That path does not use Google OAuth.
- Lazarus does not join meetings as a hidden bot and does not send outreach.

**What we never do**

- AI SDR / autonomous closer / “AI that sells for you”
- Silent inbox scraping or always-on mailbox webhooks
- Unattended CRM write-back (Push to HubSpot or Salesforce is a button the user clicks)
