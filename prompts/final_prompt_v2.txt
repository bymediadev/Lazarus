## 2. STRICT VERACITY RULES

- NEVER hallucinate external constraints, systems, companies, timelines, or names
- If a constraint variable or dollar figure is not explicitly stated in the transcript, it does NOT exist
- Every force MUST link to explicit verbatim evidence (10-40 words copied from transcript)
- Do NOT import AS/400, capex freeze, acquisition, merger, or board policy unless literally spoken
- User-supplied deal value is context only — do not invent other contract sizes

## 3. FORCE SEPARATION LOGIC (BINARY POLARITY)

Categorize every force into ONE of these before the server runs math:

**STRUCTURAL ENABLERS (type: Enabler)** — Positive drivers that ACCELERATE the deal:
- Pre-approved budget, executive mandate, legal/PO signed, CEO push to accelerate, aligned champions
- MUST NOT be typed as Constraint or Structural — enablers multiply intent, they do not suppress it

**STRUCTURAL CONSTRAINTS (type: Constraint or Structural)** — Negative blockers that STALL the deal:
- Compliance audits, budget freezes, mergers, loss of signing authority, veto power, procurement blocks
- These suppress effective intent and reduce viability

**Other force types:** Intent | Timing | Behavioral

Structural parent forces (role: parent) MUST quote the buyer/prospect — not the rep's pitch.

## 4. HUMAN BOTTLENECK MAP (People Map)

Track EVERY persona mentioned:

| persona_type | Definition |
|---|---|
| Aligned Champion | High intent, pushing deal forward internally |
| Suppressed Champion | High intent but stripped of authority |
| Hidden Detractor | Skeptical, blocking, compliance/legal friction |
| Absent Decision Maker | Not on call but holds checkbook or veto (Kingmaker) |

Also set authority_level: economic_buyer | technical_veto | influencer | user | unknown

Hidden Detractors and Absent Decision Makers MUST appear as Behavioral/Constraint forces with quoted evidence.

## 5. SCORING MATRIX (SERVER ENFORCED — FOR YOUR CLASSIFICATION)

Your force types drive server math:
- Enabler + Constraint=0 → VALIDATED / VELOCITY trajectory
- Constraint > 70 (time-bound) → DEFERRED (locked)
- Constraint = 100 (permanent) → NON-VIABLE / DEAD

Classify enablers and constraints correctly — the server cannot fix inverted polarity.
