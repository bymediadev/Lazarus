Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

Version: 1.3 · August 2026 · Aligned to Lazarus Deal Recovery (guest freemium + optional accounts)
Processor: Lazarus Deal Recovery — Lazarus Revenue Intelligence Inc., Ontario, Canada
Contact: support@getldr.ca

Trust Pack: DPA-001 · Incorporates Security Overview SEC-001 by reference

This DPA forms part of the agreement between Customer (Controller) and Lazarus Deal Recovery (Processor) for B2B use of the Lazarus Deal Recovery revenue intelligence / deal recovery service. It supplements the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

1. Roles and scope

CustomerData Controller for personal data in uploaded recordings, transcripts, and related content
Lazarus Deal RecoveryData Processor — processes personal data only on Customer’s documented instructions
ScopePer-deal transcription (when audio uploaded), AI-assisted extraction, deterministic scoring, optional account persistence of analysis outputs, optional CRM deal-link metadata, optional account-signal enrichment, rescue feedback metadata — not CRM-wide continuous monitoring or silent mailbox scraping
Guest useGuest (unauthenticated) analyses are processed to return a report and are not saved to a Customer account; limited security/abuse logs may still be retained

2. Customer instructions & recording consent

Customer is solely responsible for obtaining all legally required consents and permissions to record, upload, and analyze call content — including PIPEDA, U.S. state wiretap laws, employment agreements, and end-customer contracts.

Customer’s upload of content constitutes instruction to process that data for the service. Lazarus Deal Recovery does not verify consent. See Terms §2.

3. Processor obligations

  • Process personal data only to provide the service and as documented in this DPA
  • Not sell personal data or use it for advertising
  • Not use Customer content to train public AI models
  • Ensure personnel with data access are bound by confidentiality
  • Assist Customer with reasonable data subject requests (at Customer’s expense if excessive)
  • Delete or return data upon termination per §8 below

4. Security measures

Lazarus Deal Recovery implements technical and organizational measures described in Security Overview (SEC-001), including:

  • TLS 1.3 encryption in transit
  • AES-256 encryption at rest (infrastructure provider) for stored account data
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) tenant isolation on customer tables; signed-in saves scoped by user_id
  • Server-side-only privileged credentials (service role never exposed to browsers)
  • Grounding audit and anti-hallucination controls on AI outputs
  • Scheduled transcript retention purge (default 30 days for raw transcript text on saved records)

6. Data location

Default: North America (United States / Canada regions as configured in Supabase).
Alternative regions: available upon written agreement for enterprise customers.

7. Personal data breach

Lazarus Deal Recovery will notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer data, and will provide information reasonably available to assist Customer in meeting its regulatory obligations.

9. Audits

Upon reasonable notice, Customer may request information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA. Formal on-site audits may be conducted no more than once per year unless required by regulators, subject to confidentiality and scheduling constraints.

10. International transfers (GDPR)

Where GDPR applies, parties will execute Standard Contractual Clauses or another valid transfer mechanism upon request. Until then, this DPA reflects Processor obligations consistent with Article 28.

11. Term

This DPA remains in effect for the duration of the service agreement. Sections on confidentiality, deletion, and liability survive termination as applicable.
