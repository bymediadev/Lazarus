Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

For B2B customers · GDPR Article 28 · CCPA/CPRA service provider terms

Roles

Customer = Data Controller · Lazarus = Data Processor. Customer personal data in uploaded recordings/transcripts is processed solely to provide the service.

Processing scope

  • Transcription and analysis of sales call content
  • Storage of analysis outputs in Customer's tenant (Supabase RLS)
  • No sale of data · No use for advertising · No public model training

Security measures

  • TLS 1.3 in transit · AES-256 at rest
  • Row-Level Security tenant isolation
  • Service role keys server-side only (never in browser)
  • Access logging and principle of least privilege

Sub-processors

Supabase, Google (Gemini API), AssemblyAI (optional). Customer will be notified of material changes.

Data location

EU customers: project may be hosted in EU region (Frankfurt/Ireland) upon request. Standard US hosting available for US customers.

Breach notification

Lazarus will notify Customer without undue delay upon becoming aware of a personal data breach.

Deletion

Upon contract termination or written request, Customer data will be deleted within 30 days unless legal retention applies.

Template only — execute signed DPA with legal counsel for enterprise deals.
