1. Service description

Lazarus Deal Recovery provides revenue intelligence and deal recovery for B2B sales teams — per-deal autopsy, forecastability aids, and recovery recommendations. You submit evidence for a specific opportunity (recording and/or transcript, documents, optional mailbox threads, live session turns, field capture, CRM notes, and optional account-signal enrichment). The service uses AI-assisted extraction combined with deterministic server-side scoring to produce a report: risk signals, stakeholder map, evidence quotes, Deal Risk Index (DRI), and resuscitation recommendations.

Lazarus Deal Recovery is human-led and AI-assisted. It does not autonomously contact prospects, negotiate, or close deals. It is not an AI SDR, autonomous closer, or full CRM replacement. Optional tools (for example a Pipeline Leak Calculator) may use manual inputs. Signed-in users may save analyses to their account and link them to CRM deal identifiers; bi-directional CRM write-back is not generally available unless separately stated. Architecture and security controls are described in the Security Overview (SEC-001).
