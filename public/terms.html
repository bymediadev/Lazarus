1. Service description

Lazarus Deal Recovery provides revenue intelligence and deal recovery for B2B sales teams — per-deal autopsy, forecastability aids, and recovery recommendations. You submit evidence for a specific opportunity (recording and/or transcript, documents, optional mailbox threads, live session turns, field capture, CRM notes, and optional account-signal enrichment). The service uses AI-assisted extraction combined with deterministic server-side scoring to produce a report: risk signals, stakeholder map, evidence quotes, Deal Risk Index (DRI), and resuscitation recommendations.

Lazarus Deal Recovery is human-led and AI-assisted. It does not email buyers, write outreach, negotiate, or close deals. It does not promise that a deal will close. It is not an AI SDR, autonomous closer, or full CRM replacement. A person still decides. Optional tools (for example a Pipeline Leak Calculator) may use manual inputs. Signed-in users may save the report to their account and link it to a CRM deal. HubSpot and Salesforce stay read-only until someone pushes the plan. That push is not a full two-way sync. Architecture and security controls are described in the Security Overview (SEC-001).
