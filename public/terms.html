1. Service description

Lazarus Deal Recovery v1 provides deal autopsy and deal recovery intelligence for B2B sales teams. You upload a call recording and/or transcript for a specific opportunity. The service uses AI-assisted extraction combined with deterministic server-side scoring to produce a per-deal report: risk signals, stakeholder map, evidence quotes, Deal Risk Index (DRI), and resuscitation recommendations.

Lazarus Deal Recovery is human-led and AI-assisted. It does not autonomously contact prospects, negotiate, or close deals. It is not an AI SDR, autonomous closer, or CRM replacement. Optional tools such as the Pipeline Leak Calculator use manual inputs only and do not connect to your CRM. Architecture and security controls are described in the Security Overview (SEC-001).
