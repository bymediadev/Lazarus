B2B deal recovery software

Deal recovery software for stalled pipeline

For sales managers and VPs who own the forecast call. Human-led. AI-assisted. Not an AI SDR.

Deal recovery software is a judgment layer for sales managers. It reads the recording, transcript, email, and CRM notes you already have, then says which stalled B2B deals are recoverable versus a flat no — and what to do in the next 0–90 days. Lazarus never joins the meeting.

You can see which deals are stalled. You cannot walk into forecast knowing which will close, which still deserve effort, and which are fiction on the board. That is what deal recovery software is for.

What deal recovery software does

  • Which rep-owned deals are actually going to close
  • Which stalled deals are recoverable vs a flat no
  • The blocker, and a 0–90 day plan you paste into HubSpot or Salesforce notes

What it is not

Not a recorder. Not a meeting bot. Not ChatGPT in a wrapper. Not an autonomous closer. Keep Meet, Teams, or Zoom. Lazarus sits on top and judges the deal.

How a recovery run works

  1. Add evidence: a recording, transcript, email thread, or CRM notes, in one run.
  2. Lazarus scores the deal and names the blocker in plain language.
  3. Take the brief into forecast. Paste a short note into HubSpot or Salesforce.
