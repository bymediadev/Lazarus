Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Account information (name, email) when you sign up
  • Call recordings and transcripts you upload for analysis
  • Deal metadata (client name, deal value, analysis outputs)
  • Technical logs (IP, browser type) for security and uptime

How we process data

Uploads are transmitted over TLS 1.3 and stored encrypted at rest (AES-256 via Supabase). Analysis uses third-party AI APIs configured for zero retention / no model training on your content. Results are stored in your tenant-isolated database (Row-Level Security).

Retention & deletion

Raw audio is processed in memory and not persisted by default. Transcripts and analysis outputs are retained per your workspace settings. Recommended default: automatic deletion of raw transcript text after 30 days. You may request deletion at any time via support.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You may access, export, correct, or delete personal data. EU residents may lodge complaints with their supervisory authority. California residents may opt out of sale/sharing (we do not sell data).

Sub-processors

Supabase (database), Google Gemini (analysis), AssemblyAI (optional transcription).
