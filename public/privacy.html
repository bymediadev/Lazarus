1. Who this applies to

This Privacy Policy describes how Lazarus Deal Recovery collects and uses information when you use our B2B deal autopsy and call analysis service (Lazarus Deal Recovery v1). If you use Lazarus Deal Recovery on behalf of a company, your organization is responsible for the business content you upload; we process it as described here and in any executed Data Processing Addendum (DPA).

3. Ownership of your content

You retain ownership of recordings, transcripts, and related business content. We receive a limited license to process that content solely to provide the service, as stated in our Terms of Service. We do not sell customer content or use it to train public AI models.

4. Recording consent (critical)

You are responsible for having legal authority to upload and analyze call recordings and transcripts. Lazarus Deal Recovery does not record calls on your behalf and does not verify consent for you.

You must comply with applicable law, including:

  • PIPEDA and Canadian provincial recording rules
  • U.S. federal and state wiretap / all-party consent laws
  • Employment policies and customer or vendor contracts

See Terms of Service §2 for the full customer warranty.

5. How we process and protect data

Security controls are summarized in our Security Overview (SEC-001). At a high level:

  • In transit: TLS 1.3
  • At rest: AES-256 (Supabase infrastructure)
  • Tenant isolation: Row-Level Security (RLS) on customer data tables
  • AI processing: Third-party APIs (Google Gemini, optional AssemblyAI) configured for zero retention / no model training on your content
  • Secrets: Service credentials stored server-side only — never in the browser
