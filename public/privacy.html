This Privacy Policy describes how Lazarus Deal Recovery collects and uses information when you use our B2B revenue intelligence / deal recovery service (forecastability aids and per-deal autopsy). You may try the product as a guest (no account) or sign in with an optional account (Supabase Auth). If you use Lazarus Deal Recovery on behalf of a company, your organization is responsible for the business content you upload; we process it as described here and in any executed Data Processing Addendum (DPA).

3. Ownership of your content

You retain ownership of recordings, transcripts, and related business content. You are the controller. We are the processor. We receive a limited license to process that content solely to provide the service, as stated in our Terms of Service.

You send the call, the email, or the notes to this hosted app. We read them here to say whether the deal is recoverable or a flat no, what is blocking it, and what to do next. Scoring sends the transcript to Google Gemini. We do not save the transcript. We do not sell customer content, use it for advertising, or use it to train public AI models.

This policy describes the hosted app. A company key lets a server post a deal into that app. The call is sent here, then to Google Gemini for scoring. We save the report in the Lazarus database and we do not save the transcript. The key is shown once. We store a hash of the key, not the key itself. The key does not put the portal on your server.

A standalone install is the same app on the company’s server, writing to a database they own. Scoring on that install sends the transcript to Google Gemini with their key. The Lazarus database does not hold those rows. The company key does not create that install. This policy covers the hosted app.

5. How we process and protect data

Security controls are summarized in our Security Overview (SEC-001). At a high level:

  • In transit: TLS 1.3
  • At rest: AES-256 (Supabase infrastructure) for stored account data and saved analyses
  • Tenant isolation: Row-Level Security (RLS) on customer data tables; signed-in saves are scoped to user_id
  • AI processing: Third-party APIs (Google Gemini, optional AssemblyAI) configured for zero retention / no model training on your content where available
  • Secrets: Service credentials stored server-side only — never in the browser
