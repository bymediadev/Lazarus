Lazarus

Enterprise Security Battlecard · Deterministic Deal Intelligence · Not a note-taker

Positioning

Lazarus is a forecasting protection engine for sales leadership. It runs deterministic deal autopsies on stalled calls — force maps, People Map bottlenecks, and resuscitation scripts — without sending customer data to train public AI models.
Not competing with: Gong/Chorus recording, ChatGPT paste, or rep note tools.
Competing with: Blind pipeline, happy ears, and hour-long call forensics by VPs.
