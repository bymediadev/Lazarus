What is Lazarus Deal Recovery? (say this first)

Lazarus Deal Recovery is the layer on top — not another meeting tool. You already meet on Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or whatever your team uses. That is fine. Keep it. Lazarus Deal Recovery takes what you already captured — a recording, a transcript, emails, CRM notes — and answers the hard question: Is this deal still winnable? Who is blocking it? What do we do next? It gives you a risk score, who matters in the deal, and clear next steps. A person still runs the deal. Lazarus Deal Recovery does not replace your meetings and does not close deals for you. Your data is not used to train public AI models.
Three layers — don’t buy the same thing twice.
Layer 1 — Capture: Meet, Teams, or your existing meeting stack. Saves the call. Makes a transcript.
Layer 2 — Judgment: Lazarus Deal Recovery. Scores the deal, maps the people, spots what changed, links today to past notes.
Layer 3 — Action: Your rep and manager. Run the call. Move the deal.
Lazarus Deal Recovery is Layer 2. We are not asking you to rip out Layer 1.

One-liner: “Keep your meeting tools. Add Lazarus Deal Recovery on top. We tell you if the deal is still alive — and why.”

Why not just buy another recorder?

Meeting tools answer: “What was said?” Lazarus Deal Recovery answers: “What does it mean for the deal?” Another recorder gives you more summaries, more libraries, and more coaching clips. It does not tell your VP which deals are lying on the forecast. It does not tie Month 5 back to Month 2 when the same blocker shows up under a new name. You do not need two tools that both save calls. You need one tool that judges the deal.

Why not just use ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is good at writing. It is bad at being your scorecard. Paste the same call twice and you may get two different answers. It can make up quotes or people who were never on the call. That is fine for brainstorming. It is not fine for a forecast meeting.

Where does the information come from?

Lazarus Deal Recovery only analyzes what you authorize for that deal. You upload files, paste transcripts, search a mailbox for a specific thread, start a live meeting session, record a short field note, or import HubSpot notes. Optional WhiteWhale enrichment adds account buying signals when configured. Guests can try freemium analyses (not saved); signed-in users may persist reports to their account. There is no silent always-on inbox scrape and no secret CRM write pipe. Every score traces back to evidence you attached for that run.

How is the deal scored?

Two steps — AI reads, code scores. The Deal Risk Score is math on our server, not a chatbot guess. Same inputs produce the same score every time.
StepWhat happensRuns where
1 — ExtractAI pulls forces, people, quotes, and objections from the text you uploadedGoogle Gemini API (Layer 1)
2 — GroundServer checks every quote and stakeholder claim against the source transcript; strips invented contentLazarus Deal Recovery API (grounding.ts)
3 — ScoreFixed rules compute viability, trajectory, and Deal Risk Index (DRI) from grounded forcesLazarus Deal Recovery API (scoring.ts) — deterministic
4 — ReportPeople Map, friction changes, historical links, and recovery steps shown in UIBrowser (display only)

Deal Risk Index (plain English): A 0–100 score from deal viability, buyer pressure, lock-in, missing decision-makers, and stall signals. The formula is shown in every report.

Not used for scoring: sales playbooks, ChatGPT coaching, or training on your data. Only verified claims feed the score.

What happens if grounding fails?

We strip the lie — then we score what’s left. If the AI invents a quote, person, or template phrase that is not in your upload, the server removes it before the Deal Risk Score is calculated. The run still completes; it does not silently fail.
